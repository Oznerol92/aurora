Reusable shapes the writing leans on:

- **Hook + Number** — open with a concrete claim carrying a specific number, then a paragraph that justifies the number with a calculation.
- **Problem → Anti-patterns → Solution** — state an observed problem, list 3 documented failure modes with consequences, then the proposed solution.
- **Numbers in tables, not prose** — put real metrics in a small table (metric | value), not buried in sentences.
- **Anti-hype section** — a dedicated "When this method does NOT help you" with 2-3 specific scenarios.

Cadence: shorter sentences in narrative passages, slightly longer in analytical ones. Bullet points for scannability, but keep them dense with substance, not filler.
