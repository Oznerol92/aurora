**Avoid** (vague, hyped, or self-aggrandizing):
best practices · leverage (as a verb) · unlock · actionable · synergy · game-changer · next-level · ultimate · guru / expert / master · secret sauce / magic config.

**Prefer** (specific, evidence-flavoured):
workflow (over generic "system") · process (over ambiguous "method") · documented (over "proven") · specific (over "actual") · trade-off (over "balance") · evidence-based (over the cliché "data-driven") · preset / configuration (over "magic settings").

These are defaults, not absolutes — a user's own persona lexicon overrides them.
