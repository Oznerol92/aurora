Distributing to your OWN qualified network (e.g. a personal LinkedIn audience that chose to follow you) is allowed once per asset — distinct from invasive promo to a cold audience, which is not. Do it like a professional sharing something useful, not a creator running promo.

**Structural rules:**

- One post per asset. No re-share, no bumping with comments, no carousel of updates.
- Write natively in the network's language (the master/adaptation rule applies — don't translate from the article's other-language version).
- Publish when you have ~30 minutes to reply to first comments in the following couple of hours. No post-and-disappear.
- Link the long-form article directly in the body. Never the "link in first comment" trick (algorithms penalize it and it's contrary to a transparent voice). Never a direct storefront/paid link — the paid edition should be found through the article, not hard-sold.

**Tone:** rigorous anti-hype — no "crushes it / the secret / revolutionary / incredible", no rocket or fireworks emoji. Specific numbers, the same ones as the article (cross-channel consistency). No money-promise hooks even if true (they trigger distrust); prefer a competence-claim or method-claim. Include one inline mini-anti-hype line ("this doesn't work for X").

**Hashtags:** 1-3 maximum, specific. Stuffing 6+ generic tags signals "promotional content" and performs worse.

**Post template:** (1) hook in one sentence, no money-claim, no hype · (2) context in 2-3 lines: what you did, why it mattered, 1-2 key numbers · (3) what you learned in 3-5 lines: the core of the method, briefly · (4) one inline anti-hype line: when it does NOT serve · (5) an invitation without pressure, article link in the body · (6) optional one genuine question to the network · (7) 1-3 specific hashtags.

**Never include:** a direct paid-product link or screenshot · a "buy now / limited time" CTA · self-pity ("I'm nobody but…") · boasting about earnings.
