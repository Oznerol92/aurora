Images for a method asset are functional, not decorative. Distinct roles:

- **Article kicker** — full-width image above the headline. The one image that earns its keep: it lifts click-through meaningfully on a long-form platform. Should visualize the article's central metaphor (e.g. a stylized exploration/knowledge map for a "map the territory" piece).
- **Cover** — for a paid edition on a storefront; visible in the marketplace, so worth doing. More technical/architectural than the kicker (e.g. a blueprint of the workflow's modules with a feedback loop).
- **In-article diagram** — optional, only if stats show mid-article drop-off; a small flow diagram that anchors a key sequence.
- **Banner** — optional, horizontal, for an email funnel; subject on one third, negative space for text on the rest.

**Style direction.** Minimalist editorial illustration (think serious-publication house style or clean data-viz), hand-drawn-but-precise linework, flat not glossy. Use one tight 3-4 colour palette consistently across every asset — a dark/deep background, an off-white linework colour, one muted accent, optional muted sub-accent — so the assets read as one visual identity without needing a logo. Keep specs as guidance: roughly 16:9 for kickers, 1:1 for covers, opaque PNG preferred.

**Anti-patterns (never):** stock photos of people smiling at laptops · robots, neural-network glow, "AI brain" clichés · glossy hyperrealistic 3D renders · recognizable brand logos · garbled in-render text (add labels in post instead). If a generator can't produce something sober after a few tries, a restrained stock photo beats a poor AI render.
