A method asset usually splits into three connected pieces: a long-form article (the discovery channel), a free template/companion (zero-friction giveaway), and a paid extended edition (the monetized depth). Publish them as a funnel: article → free companion → paid edition, each cross-linked.

**Three setup tiers**, pick by available time:

- **Minimum (~45 min)** — paid edition on a storefront, free companion as a public doc/gist linking to it, article on a long-form platform linking both. Skip tags, images, promotion. Asset is live and self-promotes via search SEO.
- **Fast (~2h)** — minimum, plus optimized discovery tags, a kicker image, one cross-link pass so the three points connect.
- **Complete (~3-4h)** — fast, plus passive email capture, a social launch thread, a professional-network cross-post, and one (and only one) submission to a relevant community.

**Promotion floor**: correct discovery tags · a specific subtitle (A/B-testable) · ONE community submission, then leave it alone. Never spam, never run paid ads for a low-price asset.

**Iteration**: nothing for the first ~6 weeks (search SEO matures over months). Re-read at 3 months, datestamp updates. If a tool/dependency changes, update files with a visible "Updated [date]" note; for a major shift, publish a follow-up that links the original rather than rewriting it.

**Post-publication discipline**: answer "what was your private project?" with one short, polite, definitive line — never partial details (that opens a loop). For copies: fast takedown if needed, no public war. For refunds: grant and learn. For consulting asks: default to a polite decline pointing at the paid edition.

**Cross-language**: publish the author's stronger-market language first, the other 2-4 weeks later, footers cross-linking both.
