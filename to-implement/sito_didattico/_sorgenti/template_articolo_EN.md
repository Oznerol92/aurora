# Article template — skeleton to fill in

> **The skeleton of an article done the way we do them.** Copy this file, fill
> the `[...]` parts with your own material, delete the notes in italics. Next to
> it you'll find a *real worked example*: keep it open while you fill this in.

---

## Title

`[A concrete title. No hype, no magic promises. A title that says what you'll
learn, or a real number, beats an inflated one.]`

*Note: avoid "X money in Y days" titles. They win the click and lose anyone who
can read. Prefer a competence-claim ("What N attempts taught me about X") or a
method-claim ("A 4-step workflow for Y").*

## Subtitle

`[One line saying what it's about and who it's for. Specific, not vague.]`

---

## 1. The hook (the opening)

`[Open with a real problem — yours or the reader's. A concrete situation you've
been in. Do NOT open by explaining what the tool is: open with the need.]`

*Note: the reader must understand within three lines why they should keep going.*

## 2. The problem, with numbers

`[How much does the problem cost? Time, money, effort. Put the real numbers in.
If the classic route cost $15,000 and 4 months, say it with those numbers.]`

*Note: specific numbers always, never vague ranges like "a lot of time".*

## 3. What you did instead — the method in steps

`[The core. Lay out your method in numbered steps. Each step: what you do, why,
and the outcome. Be operational: the reader must be able to redo it.]`

1. `[Step 1 — what, why]`
2. `[Step 2 — what, why]`
3. `[Step 3 — what, why]`
4. `[Step 4 — what, why]`

## 4. The results, substantiated

`[What you got, with numbers. Plus the transparent math of the value: if you say
"I saved X", show how you get to X. No figures dropped without backing.]`

## 5. When NOT to use this method

`[Mandatory section. Say honestly when your method does NOT help, when it's
wrong, what its limits are. This is what makes you credible: anyone who promises
it always works is selling, not teaching.]`

## 6. Mistakes to avoid

`[At least three concrete anti-patterns, each with its consequence. "If you do
this, that happens." Lived, not theoretical.]`

- `[Anti-pattern 1 → consequence]`
- `[Anti-pattern 2 → consequence]`
- `[Anti-pattern 3 → consequence]`

## 7. Closing + optional pointer

`[Close without rhetoric. If you have an extended version or a template, link it
here soberly — a pointer, not an infomercial.]`

`[If you sell something: link to the product. Example format:
available here → yourname.gumroad.com/l/YOUR-PRODUCT]`

---

### Before publishing, check:

- [ ] Is there a "when NOT to use it" section?
- [ ] Is every number substantiated (clear where it comes from)?
- [ ] At least 3 documented mistakes with their consequence?
- [ ] No guru/motivational tone, no guaranteed-results promises?
- [ ] None of your personal data, or your project's, that you don't want public?
- [ ] Does the title hold up without inflating?
