Write a credible, non-hype method or experience article using the Aurora method.

1. **Plan.** Confirm the topic, audience, and publish target (ask if unknown).
   Pull the 7-section article skeleton as the spine, and keep the voice rules and
   forbidden-word list in view. Decide the single concrete hook (a real problem or
   a number), not a "what this tool is" opener.

2. **Execute.** Draft the sections in order:
   - Hook — open on a real, concrete problem or a specific number in the first
     three lines.
   - The problem, with numbers — quantify the cost (time/money/effort) in figures,
     never "a lot of time".
   - The method in numbered steps — each step states what, why, and the outcome;
     operational enough to redo.
   - Results, substantiated — show the transparent math behind any "I saved X".
   - When NOT to use this — a mandatory honest section on where it fails.
   - Mistakes to avoid — at least three concrete anti-patterns, each with its
     consequence.
   - Close — hand judgement back to the reader; any pointer is sober, not an ad.
     Write in the user's voice (persona), imitating the worked example's techniques.

3. **Verify.** Run the draft against the quality gate and forbidden-words rules:
   concrete numeric opening, a "when NOT to use" section, every number
   substantiated, ≥3 documented mistakes, no banned words, an honest non-sentimental
   close, no private/identifying specifics. Revise until it passes, then deliver.
